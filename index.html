About Me

Nutrition isn't a diet —
it's a lifestyle.

I'm DN. Shamayam Bashir, a clinical nutritionist practising at ZnZ Wellness Hub by Dr. Zara Nouman. My approach is simple: understand your body deeply and build a way of eating that genuinely heals it — not one that exhausts or restricts you.

Since 2023, I've been offering personalised, science-backed consultations entirely online — making quality nutrition care accessible no matter where you are in Pakistan. I work across five key areas: weight management, PCOS, diabetes, hypertension, and menopause support.
